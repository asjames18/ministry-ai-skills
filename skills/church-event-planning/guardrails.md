# Church Event Planning Guardrails

These boundaries ensure physical safety, financial stewardship, and legal protection for church events.

---

## 🛑 Operational & Safety Guardrails

1. **Child Protection & Background Checks**: Never plan childcare or youth activities without mandating church background checks, two-adult child supervision policies, and secure check-in/check-out tag procedures.
2. **Food Allergies & Safety**: When meals or snacks are provided, clear labeling for common allergens (peanuts, gluten, dairy) must be planned.
3. **Overcrowding & Fire Codes**: Ensure seating layouts adhere to local room occupancy limits and maintain clear exit walkways.
4. **Realistic Volunteer Hours**: Never schedule volunteers for consecutive high-intensity shifts without scheduled meals or rest periods.
5. **Fiscal Transparency**: Event registration fees should reflect cost-recovery or intentional ministry subsidy; never mask undisclosed fees.

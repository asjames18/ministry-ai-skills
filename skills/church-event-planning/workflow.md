# Church Event Planning Workflow

This document guides ministry teams on how to use this skill from event concept through post-event evaluation.

---

## 🔄 Event Life Cycle

```text
[Concept & Date Booking] ──> Run Church Event Planning Skill
                                      │
                                      ▼
[Committee / Staff Review] ──> Refine Master Run-of-Show & Budget
                                      │
                                      ▼
[6-Week Promotional Runway] ──> Launch Registration, Recruit Volunteers
                                      │
                                      ▼
[Event Week Logistics Check] ──> A/V, Facilities, Safety Walkthrough
                                      │
                                      ▼
[Event Day Execution] ──> Volunteer Prayer Huddle, Run-of-Show
                                      │
                                      ▼
[Post-Event Follow-up] ──> Thank-you notes, survey, debrief meeting
```

---

## 📋 Step-by-Step Procedure

### 1. Initial Parameters Setup
- Gather key goals, date constraints, target room reservations, and estimated budget.

### 2. Generate the Blueprint
- Run the `church-event-planning` prompt with your event parameters to get the run-of-show, promotion schedule, and volunteer roster.

### 3. Delegate to Team Leads
- Share the Run-of-Show with your Production/AV lead.
- Assign the Volunteer Matrix to your Hospitality / Volunteer Coordinator.
- Hand the 6-week Promotion schedule to your Communications team.

### 4. Event Day Execution
- Print the Master Run-of-Show for all stage hosts, audio techs, and lead volunteers.
- Hold a 15-minute Volunteer Prayer and Briefing Huddle 45 minutes before doors open.

### 5. Debrief & Celebrate
- Send thank-you emails to all volunteers within 48 hours.
- Review attendance metrics and attendee feedback.

# Church Event Planning Skill 🎪

> **Generate comprehensive event execution blueprints: run-of-show schedules, volunteer role rosters, multi-week promotion timelines, facility checklists, and budget allocations.**

---

## 🎯 Purpose & Overview
Executing a successful church event—whether a community outreach, summer VBS, marriage retreat, youth camp, or volunteer appreciation banquet—requires dozens of moving pieces.

This skill equips event directors and ministry volunteers with an end-to-end operational blueprint:
1. **Minute-by-Minute Run-of-Show**: Exact timeline from setup to teardown with lead owners.
2. **Volunteer Role Matrix**: Clear job descriptions, shift times, and volunteer-to-attendee ratio recommendations.
3. **6-Week Promotion & Communications Timeline**: Phased communication across pulpit, email, social, and print.
4. **Facility & Tech Checklist**: Audio/Visual, lighting, seating configurations, safety/first aid, and child check-in needs.
5. **Budget Framework & Risk Mitigation**: Expense categorization and contingency planning for weather, medical, or crowd overflow.

---

## 👥 Target Users
- Church Event Coordinators & Ministry Directors
- Youth & Children's Ministry Pastors
- Men's / Women's Ministry Leaders
- Volunteer Hospitality Leads & Facilities Managers

---

## 📥 Inputs Required
- **Event Name & Purpose**: What is the event and its core spiritual/community goal?
- **Date, Time & Duration**: Target date, start/end time, setup windows.
- **Estimated Attendance & Audience**: Expected number of attendees and demographics.
- **Budget & Location**: Budget constraints and physical venue details.
- **Key Program Elements**: Special speakers, meals, games, worship, breakout rooms.

---

## 📤 Outputs Generated
- **1. Master Run-of-Show Schedule** (Setup, live program, teardown)
- **2. Volunteer Assignment & Role Breakdown**
- **3. Phased 6-Week Promotion & Registration Plan**
- **4. Facilities, A/V, and Safety Checklist**
- **5. Budget Allocation & Risk Management Plan**

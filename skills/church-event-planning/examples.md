# Church Event Planning Examples

This document demonstrates realistic input data and sample event plans generated by the Church Event Planning skill.

---

## 📌 Example 1: Marriage Date Night & Workshop

### Input Payload
```json
{
  "event_name": "ReConnect: Marriage Date Night & Workshop",
  "purpose_and_goals": "Provide married couples an encouraging, Christ-centered date night with practical communication tools, dinner, and quality conversation.",
  "target_date_and_time": "Friday, November 13, 2026 | 6:30 PM - 9:00 PM",
  "location_and_spaces": "Church Worship Center (tables set in rounds) & Fellowship Hall (buffet)",
  "expected_attendance": 120,
  "target_audience": "Engaged and married couples (all ages)",
  "total_budget": "$2,200 (partially offset by $25/couple ticket)",
  "program_elements": [
    "Catered Italian dinner",
    "Fun icebreaker couple game",
    "Keynote talk on 'Grace in Everyday Communication' by guest speaker",
    "Guided table discussion & couple reflection worksheet",
    "Dessert & coffee bar with live acoustic music"
  ]
}
```

### Generated Output

```markdown
# 🎪 ReConnect: Marriage Date Night Master Plan
**Date:** Friday, November 13, 2026 | 6:30 PM – 9:00 PM  
**Venue:** Worship Center (Dining Setup) & Fellowship Hall (Buffet)  
**Expected Attendance:** 120 (60 couples)  
**Budget:** $2,200  

---

| Time | Duration | Segment / Activity | Lead Role | Notes & Tech Cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| 5:30 PM | 30 min | Catering Delivery & Warmers | Hospitality Lead | Italian pasta buffet, gluten-free option marked |
| 5:45 PM | 15 min | A/V & Sound Check | Media Lead | Test wireless handheld mics, playlist, slides |
| 6:00 PM | 15 min | **Volunteer Prayer & Huddle** | Event Coordinator | Brief team, pray for marriages, hand out lanyards |
| 6:15 PM | 15 min | Doors Open / Check-in | Welcome Greeters | Background jazz playlist, give each couple a seating card |
| 6:30 PM | 10 min | Welcome & Opening Prayer | Host Pastor | Welcome couples, explain evening flow, pray over food |
| 6:40 PM | 35 min | Dinner Buffet Open | Food Service Team | Release tables 3 at a time to prevent long lines |

### 2. Volunteer Staffing Matrix (Total: 18 Volunteers)

1. **Check-In & Greeter Team (4 volunteers)**: 
   - *Shift*: 6:00 PM – 7:00 PM
   - *Duties*: Greet couples warmly, check registrations, hand out table assignments and discussion packets.
2. **Food Service & Hospitality (6 volunteers)**: 
   - *Shift*: 5:30 PM – 9:15 PM
   - *Duties*: Monitor buffet warmers, refill water carafes, manage dessert bar and coffee station.
3. **Audio/Visual & Lighting Tech (2 volunteers)**: 
   - *Shift*: 5:15 PM – 9:15 PM
   - *Duties*: Run microphone levels, control presentation slides, manage house lighting transitions.
4. **Childcare Team (Offered as add-on in Kids Wing) (4 vetted volunteers)**: 
   - *Shift*: 6:00 PM – 9:15 PM
   - *Duties*: Secure check-in/out, supervise pizza and movie night for kids ages 3–11.
5. **Teardown & Cleanup Team (4 volunteers)**: 
   - *Shift*: 8:45 PM – 9:45 PM
   - *Duties*: Wipe down tables, empty trash to dumpster, reset sanctuary for Sunday.

---

### 3. Phased 6-Week Promotion Timeline

- **Phase 1: 6 to 4 Weeks Out (Oct 2 - Oct 16)**:
  - Launch registration page with early-bird discount ($20/couple).
  - First stage announcement focusing on investing in marriages.
  - Recruit catering and hospitality volunteers.
- **Phase 2: 3 to 2 Weeks Out (Oct 17 - Oct 30)**:
  - Feature video promo from guest speaker in service and on Instagram.
  - Email blast with "3 Reasons to Take a Marriage Night Off."
- **Phase 3: 1 Week Out (Nov 1 - Nov 11)**:
  - "Final 15 Tickets Left" urgency push. Registration closes Wednesday at midnight for catering count.
  - Send "What to Expect" prep email to registered couples (childcare info, parking tips).
- **Phase 4: Post-Event (Nov 14 - Nov 18)**:
  - Send thank-you email with the speaker's discussion notes and book recommendation.
  - Highlight photos in Sunday announcements; link to marriage small group signups.

---

### 4. Logistics, Tech & Safety Checklist

- [ ] **A/V**: 2 wireless handhelds + 1 lavalier mic, Spotify playlist queued, presentation slides loaded.
- [ ] **Sanctuary Setup**: 15 round tables, black tablecloths, warm LED votive candles, table numbers.
- [ ] **Childcare Room**: Two CPR-certified adults, check-in printer verified, allergy list posted on door.
- [ ] **Signage**: Directional signs in lobby pointing to "Check-In" and "Childcare Wing."

---

### 5. Budget Allocation & Risk Contingency

**Budget Breakdown ($2,200 total):**
- Catering & Food/Drinks: $1,200 (54%)
- Guest Speaker Honorarium & Travel: $500 (23%)
- Table Decor & Tablecloths: $200 (9%)
- Print Materials & Pens: $100 (5%)
- Contingency Reserve (10%): $200 (9%)

**Contingency Mitigation:**
- *Catering Overrun*: Order buffet for 130 to guarantee 10-person buffer.
- *Childcare Surge*: Cap childcare registrations 5 days prior to guarantee strict 1:5 worker-to-child ratio.
```

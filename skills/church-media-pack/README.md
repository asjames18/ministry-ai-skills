# Church Media Pack Skill 🎨

> **Generate cohesive creative assets for Sunday: AI image prompts (Midjourney/DALL-E), sermon slide copy, Instagram carousels, video hooks, and YouTube metadata.**

---

## 🎯 Purpose & Overview
Creative and media teams often face tight weekly deadlines to produce graphics, sermon series branding, social media promotional teasers, video hooks for short-form video (Reels, TikTok, Shorts), and presentation slides.

This skill equips church media teams, solo volunteers, and graphic designers with a complete creative media kit:
1. **AI Image Generation Prompts**: Optimized text-to-image prompts (for Midjourney v6, DALL-E 3, Adobe Firefly) designed for sermon bumpers, title graphics, and backgrounds.
2. **Sermon Title Slide Copy & Lower Thirds**: High-impact text layouts formatted for screen readability.
3. **Instagram Carousel Script (5 Slides)**: Engaging discipleship-focused swipe-through cards.
4. **Short-Form Video Hooks & Scripts (15-30s Reels/Shorts)**: High-retention hooks and audio concepts.
5. **YouTube / Podcast Metadata**: Click-worthy, respectful titles, descriptions, and chapter timestamps.

---

## 👥 Target Users
- Church Media Directors & Graphic Designers
- Social Media Managers & Content Volunteers
- Video Editors & Livestream Directors
- Pastors producing digital devotionals or podcasts

---

## 📥 Inputs Required
- **Sermon or Series Title**: Main theme or title (e.g., *Restless Heart: Finding Peace in an Anxious World*).
- **Key Scripture Passage**: Main text (e.g., *Philippians 4:4-9*).
- **Core Big Idea / Takeaway**: 1-2 sentence core message.
- **Visual Aesthetic & Mood**: (e.g., Modern minimalist, Warm cinematic film, Deep moody dramatic, Vibrant and energetic).
- **Target Channels**: (e.g., Instagram, YouTube, ProPresenter, TikTok).

---

## 📤 Outputs Generated
- **1. Text-to-Image Generation Prompts** (Midjourney & DALL-E 3 prompts with aspect ratios `--ar 16:9` and `--ar 1:1`)
- **2. Screen Slide Text & Lower-Third Templates**
- **3. 5-Slide Instagram Educational Carousel Copy**
- **4. 2 Short-Form Video Concepts & Hooks (Reels / TikTok / YouTube Shorts)**
- **5. YouTube Title & SEO Description Block**

# Church Media Pack Guardrails

These boundaries ensure that visual media and social content remain Christ-honoring, tasteful, and legally sound.

---

## 🛑 Guardrails & Standards

1. **No Irreverent, Bizarre, or Distracting Visuals**: Avoid grotesque AI artifacts (extra fingers, unnatural faces) or irreverent depictions of sacred subjects. Always vet AI images before projecting them on sanctuary screens.
2. **Text Readability & High Contrast**: Presentation screens must be easily readable from the back row of the auditorium. Avoid busy backgrounds directly behind small text.
3. **No Sensationalist Clickbait**: Video hooks and YouTube titles must accurately reflect the biblical message. Do not use fear-mongering, outrage, or deceptive titles to drive clicks.
4. **Respect Copyright & Licensing**: Ensure all AI-generated images comply with the terms of service of the generator tool, and never attempt to reproduce copyrighted logos or trademarked artwork.
5. **Human Representation**: Ensure social and media imagery reflects a diverse, authentic representation of the body of Christ without using fake, hyper-glamorized depictions.

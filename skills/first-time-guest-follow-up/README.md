# First-Time Guest Follow-Up Skill 🤝

> **Create warm, authentic, low-pressure follow-up communications across SMS, email, and handwritten notes for Sunday visitors.**

---

## 🎯 Purpose & Overview
Visiting a new church can be intimidating. A timely, genuine, and hospitable follow-up makes visitors feel valued without feeling overwhelmed or bombarded.

This skill equips hospitality pastors, church administrators, and welcome teams with an organized, multi-touch follow-up sequence:
1. **Sunday Afternoon SMS**: A quick, 2-sentence warm thank-you text with no pressure.
2. **Tuesday Welcome Email**: A personalized message from the pastor with links to learn more, meet leaders, and ask questions.
3. **Pastor / Welcome Team Handwritten Card Template**: A short 3-4 sentence message suitable for physical post cards.
4. **Thursday "What's Next" Touchpoint**: A gentle invitation to the upcoming Sunday or a casual "Coffee with the Pastor" event.
5. **Internal Volunteer Care Notes**: Guidance for follow-up callers or pastoral team check-ins.

---

## 👥 Target Users
- Assimilation Pastors & Connections Directors
- Church Administrators & ChMS Operators (Planning Center, Breeze, CCB)
- Welcome Desk & First Impressions Volunteers
- Lead Pastors

---

## 📥 Inputs Required
- **Guest Context**: First name, attendance mode (in-person vs. online), family status (with kids, solo, couple), interests indicated on connection card.
- **Church Identity**: Church name, lead pastor name, website URL.
- **Upcoming Next Step**: Next Sunday's sermon topic or next upcoming newcomer event.

*(See [`inputs.schema.json`](inputs.schema.json) for details)*

---

## 📤 Outputs Generated
- **1. Day 0 (Sunday Afternoon) SMS** (under 160 characters, natural, low-pressure)
- **2. Day 2 (Tuesday) Pastoral Welcome Email** (Warm, 150-200 words, clear links)
- **3. Handwritten Postcard Script** (Short, heartfelt, pen-ready)
- **4. Day 5 (Thursday/Friday) Weekend Preview SMS or Email**
- **5. Internal Hospitality Triage Note** (Action checklist for staff/volunteers)

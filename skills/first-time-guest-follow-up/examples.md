# First-Time Guest Follow-Up Examples

This document demonstrates realistic input data and sample follow-up sequences generated by the First-Time Guest Follow-Up skill.

---

## 📌 Example 1: Family with Children Visiting In-Person

### Input Payload
```json
{
  "church_name": "Living Hope Church",
  "lead_pastor_name": "Pastor Marcus",
  "visitor_first_name": "Sarah",
  "attendance_type": "in-person",
  "family_status": "Attended with husband and two elementary-aged children who went to HopeKids",
  "stated_interests": [
    "Kids Ministry",
    "Family Small Groups"
  ],
  "upcoming_event": "Newcomer Pizza Meet & Greet next Sunday, Oct 25 at 12:15 PM in the Community Room",
  "website_or_contact_link": "livinghope.org/welcome"
}
```

### Generated Output

```markdown
# 🤝 First-Time Guest Follow-Up Pack for Sarah
**Church:** Living Hope Church  
**Sender:** Pastor Marcus  

---

### 1. Day 0 (Sunday Afternoon) SMS
> "Hi Sarah! Pastor Marcus here from Living Hope. Just wanted to say thank you so much for joining us with your family today! Hope you have a wonderful Sunday afternoon."

---

### 2. Day 2 (Tuesday Morning) Pastoral Welcome Email

**Subject Line Options:**
1. Wonderful meeting you this Sunday, Sarah!
2. Thank you for visiting Living Hope Church (+ a quick hello)

**Email Body:**
Hi Sarah,

I wanted to send a quick personal note to say thank you for spending your Sunday morning with us at Living Hope!

Visiting a new church can feel a bit overwhelming, so we truly appreciate you taking the time to join us. I also hope your kids had a fantastic time in HopeKids! Our children's team loves creating a fun, safe space where kids can learn about Jesus.

You mentioned an interest in kids ministry and family small groups—we would love to help you and your family find genuine community here. If you ever have any questions, want to explore getting connected, or just want to grab a coffee, my door is always open.

We are also hosting a casual **Newcomer Pizza Meet & Greet this Sunday, Oct 25 at 12:15 PM** in the Community Room right after service. It's totally free, kids are welcome, and it's a great way to meet a few friendly faces without any pressure.

You can learn more anytime at [livinghope.org/welcome](https://livinghope.org/welcome).

Praying blessings over your home and family this week!

Warmly,  
**Pastor Marcus & The Living Hope Team**

---

### 3. Handwritten Postcard Script (For Welcome Volunteer)

> *"Dear Sarah & family,  
> It was such a joy having you worship with us this past Sunday at Living Hope! We hope you felt warmly welcomed and at home. If we can pray for you or serve your family in any way, please let us know. We hope to see you again soon!  
> — Your Friends at Living Hope"*

---

### 4. Day 5 (Friday) Weekend Preview SMS

> *"Hi Sarah! Hope you had a great week. We're continuing our series in Philippians this Sunday at 10 AM, followed by our Newcomer Pizza Lunch at 12:15. We'd love to see you again if you're free! Details: livinghope.org/welcome"*

---
```

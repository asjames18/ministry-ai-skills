# First-Time Guest Follow-Up Guardrails

These boundaries preserve visitor trust and dignity.

---

## 🛑 Guardrails & Ethics

1. **No High-Pressure Tactics**: Never demand that a visitor commit to membership, financial giving, or weekly volunteer shifts in initial follow-ups.
2. **Strict Anonymity & PII Protection**: Never include full addresses, phone numbers, or private prayer requests in unvetted prompt executions.
3. **Respect Opt-Outs**: If a guest requests "no contact" or opts out of SMS, respect it immediately in your ChMS.
4. **No Artificial Deception**: Do not pretend an automated mass text is a spontaneous personal phone call. Be honest, gracious, and authentic.
5. **Protect Children's Information**: Never mention children's full names or ages in external communications.

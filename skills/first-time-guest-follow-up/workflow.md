# First-Time Guest Follow-Up Workflow

This document details the multi-day assimilation workflow for welcoming first-time church guests.

---

## 📅 The 7-Day Follow-Up Rhythm

```text
[Sunday Service]     ──> Guest fills out digital or physical Connect Card
[Sunday 2:00 PM]     ──> Volunteer/Staff sends Day 0 SMS
[Monday Morning]     ──> Database entry in ChMS; run Follow-Up Skill Prompt
[Tuesday 10:00 AM]   ──> Automated or pastor-reviewed Welcome Email sent
[Wednesday]          ──> Volunteer writes and mails physical welcome postcard
[Thursday / Friday]  ──> Weekend Preview message sent (SMS or email)
[Sunday + 7 Days]    ──> Welcome team looks out for returning visitor in lobby
```

---

## 📋 Operational Instructions

### 1. Data Collection & Privacy Safeguards
- Collect visitor connection cards from Sunday.
- **Privacy Check**: Do not enter sensitive prayer requests into public AI chats. Only supply first name, general family status, and ministry interest checkboxes.

### 2. Run the Skill
- Generate the customized message sequence using `prompt.md`.

### 3. Review & Personalize
- Review the generated email and SMS to ensure any specific questions asked by the visitor are addressed with personal warmth.

### 4. Record in ChMS
- Log communication touchpoints in Planning Center People, Breeze, or Church Community Builder to prevent duplicate contacts from different staff members.

# Weekly Announcements Examples

"Good morning, Cornerstone family! It is wonderful to worship with you today. If you are joining us for the first time, an extra warm welcome—we’re so glad you're here.

As you head into this week, there’s one big date we want everyone to mark on your calendars: our **Annual Fall Family Festival** on **Saturday, October 31st from 4:00 to 7:00 PM**! *[PAUSE]* 

This is one of our favorite outreach events of the year. We're filling the back lawn with hayrides, games, food trucks, and a massive trunk-or-treat for the entire neighborhood. 

Here's how you can be part of blessing our community:
First, bring unopened bags of individually wrapped candy and drop them in the blue bins in the lobby. 
Second, we need 25 volunteers to host trunks and help run games! You can sign up right now on the **Cornerstone App** or stop by the **Info Desk** in the lobby after service.

For all our other upcoming events—including the Men’s Breakfast this Saturday and our next New Members Class—check out the printed bulletin or tap the Church Hub on your app.

Let’s welcome Pastor David as we open God's Word together!"

# Weekly Announcements Guardrails

These guidelines ensure that announcements remain welcoming, ethical, and clear.

---

## 🛑 Guardrails & Prohibitions

1. **Avoid Information Overload on Stage**: Never script more than 2 full announcements for verbal delivery. Announcing 5+ events from the stage creates cognitive overload and causes the congregation to retain nothing.
2. **No High-Pressure Giving Appeals**: For offering announcements, focus on grateful stewardship and partnering with God's work. Never use guilt, manipulation, or promises of financial return.
3. **No Private Member Data in Public Announcements**: Do not announce private medical details, full addresses for home meetings (use "RSVP for address"), or private phone numbers without consent.
4. **No Christianese Jargon**: Ensure all announcements are accessible and understandable to first-time guests who may not know church acronyms or insider terminology.
5. **No Broken Links**: Always ensure CTAs point to a single unified digital location (e.g., church app, central link hub) or physical location (Info Desk).

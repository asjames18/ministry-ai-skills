# Weekly Announcements Skill 📢

> **Turn messy, fragmented ministry notes into clear, multi-channel weekly church announcements across print, stage, email, and digital screens.**

---

## 🎯 Purpose & Overview
Every week, church administrators and communications volunteers receive unstructured requests from ministry leaders: youth group trips, women's retreats, men's breakfast, volunteer calls, and special offerings. 

This skill standardizes and transforms raw announcement submissions into:
1. **Host Stage Scripts**: Conversational, timed scripts for the Sunday morning announcement host or video team.
2. **Printed Bulletin Blurbs**: Compact, scannable summaries for print programs or digital bulletins.
3. **Weekly Email Newsletter Digest**: Warm, formatted newsletter copy for Mailchimp, Substack, or Flocknote.
4. **Slide Screen Copy**: 2-3 line punchy text designed for ProPresenter, Proclaim, or PowerPoint slides.
5. **Social Media Snippets**: Ready-to-post announcements for Instagram/Facebook with emojis and clear call-to-actions (CTAs).

---

## 👥 Target Users
- Church Administrators & Office Managers
- Sunday Service Hosts / Welcome Team Leads
- Media & Communications Directors / Volunteers
- Pastors & Ministry Department Leaders

---

## 📥 Inputs Required
- **Church Name & Personality**: Name and overarching tone (e.g., warm, energetic, traditional).
- **Raw Announcement Items**: A list of events/initiatives with dates, times, locations, contact person/link, and descriptions.
- **Priority Ranking / Focus Item**: Which single item is the top priority for stage mention.
- **Target Sunday / Date Range**: The upcoming weekend date.

*(See [`inputs.schema.json`](inputs.schema.json) for full validation details)*

---

## 📤 Outputs Generated
- **1. Verbal Stage Host Script** (60–90 seconds max, conversational, natural pauses, single clear CTA)
- **2. Printed Bulletin / Digital Program Section** (Concise 40–60 word blurbs with date/time/action)
- **3. Weekly Email Digest Section** (Engaging subject line, warm intro, structured body, next steps)
- **4. Presentation Slide Text** (Headline, Sub-headline, Key Details in under 20 words)
- **5. Social Media Copy** (Post caption, hashtag suggestions, story/reel idea)

---

## ⚡ Quick Start
1. Copy the system prompt in [`prompt.md`](prompt.md).
2. Paste into ChatGPT or Claude with your weekly event list.
3. Review, tweak details, and hand off to the Sunday host and media team!

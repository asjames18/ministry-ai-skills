# Sermon Preparation Support Skill 📖

> **A research, context, illustration, and application partner for pastors, preachers, and Bible teachers.**

---

## 🎯 Purpose & Overview
Pastors and Bible teachers often spend dozens of hours every week researching historical contexts, cross-referencing passages, and thinking through engaging illustrations and small group discussion questions.

This skill is designed as an **exegetical and homiletical research assistant**. It does **not** write sermons for the pastor to read verbatim. Instead, it provides:
1. **Historical & Cultural Context**: Background on author, original recipients, historical situation, and literary genre.
2. **Key Theological Themes & Cross-References**: Connected Old and New Testament passages and biblical theology arcs.
3. **Key Terms & Original Language Insights**: Basic lexical background on notable Greek/Hebrew terms (always clearly framed for pastoral review).
4. **Illustration Concepts**: Real-world analogies, historical anecdotes, and modern parallels.
5. **Practical Life Applications**: Age-appropriate, actionable takeaways for Monday morning living.
6. **Small Group Discussion Questions**: 4-6 questions moving from observation to interpretation to personal application.

---

## 👥 Target Users
- Senior Pastors & Teaching Pastors
- Associate / Youth Pastors
- Bi-vocational Preachers & Lay Leaders
- Sunday School Teachers & Bible Study Leaders

---

## 📥 Inputs Required
- **Scripture Passage**: The book, chapter, and verses (e.g., *Ephesians 2:1-10*).
- **Preferred Bible Translation**: (e.g., ESV, NIV, CSB, NASB, NLT, KJV).
- **Theological Tradition**: (e.g., Reformed, Wesleyan/Methodist, Baptist, Pentecostal, Anglican, Non-denominational).
- **Sermon Series / Central Big Idea**: (Optional context).
- **Target Audience & Tone**: (e.g., young professionals, multi-generational, unchurched seekers).

---

## 📤 Outputs Generated
- **1. Exegetical & Historical Background Brief**
- **2. Core Theological Points & Canonical Connections**
- **3. Original Language Term Spotlights (Greek/Hebrew transliteration + context)**
- **4. 3-4 Creative Illustration Angles**
- **5. Concrete Life Applications (Head, Heart, Hands)**
- **6. Small Group Discussion & Reflection Guide**

# Sermon Preparation Support Workflow

This document outlines how preachers and teachers can integrate this AI skill into their weekly rhythm of prayer, study, and sermon crafting.

---

## 📅 Recommended Preacher Study Cycle

```text
[Monday - Tuesday]   ──> Personal Scripture immersion, translation reading, personal prayer
[Tuesday Afternoon] ──> Run the Sermon Prep Support Skill Prompt
[Wednesday]         ──> Exegetical review, commentary study, pastoral outline synthesis
[Thursday]          ──> Drafting sermon manuscript/notes, choosing illustrations
[Friday - Saturday] ──> Internalization, prayer for the congregation, slide preparation
[Sunday Morning]    ──> Preach with spiritual conviction and dependence on the Holy Spirit
```

---

## 📋 Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Personal Study First
- Do not start with the AI. Read the passage multiple times in your physical Bible or digital study software (Logos, Accordance, Blue Letter Bible).
- Write down your initial impressions, questions, and conviction points.

### Step 2: Run the Research Assistant
- Pass the Scripture reference and your initial notes to the Sermon Prep Support prompt.

### Step 3: Verify & Synthesize
- **Cross-check Cross-references**: Look up each cited verse to confirm accuracy.
- **Review Word Studies**: Verify Greek/Hebrew root claims with trusted commentaries.
- **Select Illustrations**: Filter illustrations through your congregation's cultural context and your personal authentic voice.

### Step 4: Small Group Handoff
- Send the generated discussion questions to your small group leaders, discipleship pastors, or Sunday school teachers.

## 📋 Step-by-Step Procedure

### Step 1: Input Gathering
- The ministry leader or volunteer collects the necessary details (dates, notes, passages, event forms).

### Step 2: Privacy & Sanitization Check
- Confirm that no un-redacted personal phone numbers, sensitive medical details, or private pastoral notes are present in the raw input.

### Step 3: AI Processing
- Feed the structured inputs into the prompt using your preferred interface (ChatGPT, Claude, custom script, or automation pipeline).

### Step 4: Pastoral & Theological Review
- Check all Scripture citations and doctrinal statements.
- Ensure the tone matches the congregation's culture.
- Adjust any awkward phrasing or incorrect dates/times.

### Step 5: Distribution & Archiving
- Push the approved content to its intended destination (bulletin, website, stage presenter, email newsletter, social media).
